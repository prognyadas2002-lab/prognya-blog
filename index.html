Cafe O Kobita: A Cosy Café in the Heart of Shyambazar

📍 Location: Shyambazar, Kolkata ✨ Vibe: Aesthetic & Cozy ⭐ Rating: 4.1 / 5 💰 Price for Two: ₹200–₹600

Cafe O Kobita is one of those small cafes that feels instantly comfortable. Located just about a minute’s walk from Shyambazar Metro, it is an easy spot to visit when exploring North Kolkata or simply looking for a relaxed place to enjoy good food.

What makes this cafe particularly charming is its atmosphere. The space has a cosy, aesthetic and distinctly North Kolkata character, with a bookish ambience that connects beautifully with Bengal’s literary and cultural roots.

The food is another highlight. The menu includes different options such as pizza, fish and chips, and beverages. I personally tried the pizza and lasagna and both were genuinely good. The portion sizes were also generous, which made the experience particularly satisfying considering the price range.

For me, Cafe O Kobita captures something special about North Kolkata cafes. There is a traditional cultural character here that feels different from many newer café spaces elsewhere in the city. It feels contemporary enough to be aesthetic and comfortable, while still retaining a connection to the roots and character of North Kolkata.

If you are looking for an aesthetic and cosy café near Shyambazar Metro, with good food, generous portions and reasonable pricing, Cafe O Kobita is definitely worth a visit.

Final Verdict

My Rating: 4.1/5

A charming North Kolkata café that combines good food, generous portions, a cosy atmosphere and a distinctly Bengali cultural character — all at a reasonable price.

Shyambazar, Kolkata • Traditional & Heritage

Cafe at Calcutta: Heritage Veranda Café in Shyambazar

📍 Location: Shyambazar, North Kolkata 🏛️ Vibe: Traditional & Heritage ⭐ Rating: 4.1 / 5 💰 Price for Two: ₹200–₹600

Cafe at Calcutta is a hidden gem tucked inside a beautifully restored Bonedi Bari in Shyambazar, North Kolkata. Stepping into its airy veranda feels like traveling back in time to the golden era of North Kolkata adda and culture.

The ambience is authentically heritage and nostalgic. Wooden shutters, vintage courtyard lighting, and traditional Bengali architectural accents give this café a unique, timeless personality.

On the menu, their signature refreshing Mojito is an absolute must-try, perfectly paired with crispy Bengali evening snacks and continental bites. The portion sizes are generous and priced reasonably between ₹200–₹600 for two people.

What sets Cafe at Calcutta apart is how seamlessly it blends old-world heritage charm with casual modern café culture. It's a wonderful spot for quiet reading, casual conversations, or an unhurried afternoon coffee in North Kolkata.

If you are looking for a traditional heritage café near Shyambazar Metro with classic Bonedi Bari architecture, great signature mojitos, and authentic Bengali character, Cafe at Calcutta is well worth a visit.

Final Verdict

My Rating: 4.1/5

A delightful North Kolkata heritage café combining traditional Bonedi Bari architecture, refreshing signature mojitos, and authentic adda culture at budget-friendly prices.

Park Street, Kolkata • Heritage Colonial Dining

Peter Cat, Park Street: Beyond the Famous Chelo Kebab

📍 Location: Park Street, Central Kolkata 🏛️ Vibe: Traditional & Heritage ⭐ Rating: 3.8 / 5 💰 Price for Two: ₹800–₹1500

I visited Peter Cat last year, and honestly, the first thing I noticed was how crowded it was. But then again, it is Peter Cat on Park Street. So I was not really surprised.

The ambience is actually one of the things I liked about the place. There is a British-colonial feel to it, with wooden flooring, warm lighting and old-school music playing in the background. It has that classic Park Street feeling. You can actually feel that you are sitting in one of those old Kolkata restaurants that have been part of the city for years.

Of course, I had to try the Chelo Kebab. That is what Peter Cat is famous for. The dish has Persian or Iranian roots and comes as a complete meal with kebabs, buttered rice, egg and a lot of butter.

But personally, I found the Chelo Kebab just okay.

Maybe it was a little too buttery for me. The kebab also felt slightly chewy and dry. I know people absolutely love it and I can understand why it is so famous. If you are visiting Peter Cat for the first time, I would still say try it once. But for me, it was not the best thing I had there.

What actually surprised me was the Mutton Rogan Josh.

After finishing the Chelo Kebab, I started enjoying the Rogan Josh much more. It was flavourful and really satisfying. I honestly was not expecting it to be that good.

So if you ask me what I would recommend at Peter Cat apart from the famous Chelo Kebab, I would definitely say Mutton Rogan Josh.

I also tried their biryani. It was quite good and the portion size was generous. I liked the taste. My only complaint was the mutton pieces. I expected them to be a little bigger. It is not something I would compare with Kolkata's famous biryani specialists, but still, the biryani at Peter Cat is worth trying.

I also had a Mutton Naan with the meal.

Overall, I feel Peter Cat is better when you explore beyond the Chelo Kebab. The Chelo Kebab is obviously the dish everyone talks about. But there are other dishes on the menu that deserve attention too.

For me, the Mutton Rogan Josh was the real highlight.

And then there is the whole Peter Cat experience. The wooden floor, the music, the old-school ambience and the crowd. Everything together somehow defines Park Street for me.

I think everyone should visit Peter Cat at least once. Not necessarily only for the Chelo Kebab. Go there to experience the restaurant itself and try some of the other dishes too.

My Verdict

My rating: 3.8/5

Would I recommend the Chelo Kebab? Yes. You should try it once because it is such an iconic Peter Cat dish.

Would I order it again? Probably not my first choice.

My personal recommendation would be the Mutton Rogan Josh. I would also try the biryani and explore some of their other dishes.

For me, Peter Cat is not just about the Chelo Kebab. It is about the whole Park Street experience.
